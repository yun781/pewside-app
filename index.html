Frequently asked questions

Do I need an account?

No. Open the app, choose your Bible translation and tap Record.

What’s free?

Unlimited recording, live text, verse pop-ups, notes, the Bible and sharing your notes as text are always free.

What does Pro include?

Sermon summaries, small group questions, PDF export and importing audio files. Pro is a yearly or monthly subscription and includes Family Sharing.

Does live text work without internet?

Yes. Speech is turned into text on your iPhone. The first time, your iPhone may download Apple’s speech model over Wi‑Fi. After that, recording and live text work offline.

Why wasn’t a verse recognized?

Pewside looks for spoken references like “John three sixteen” or “Romans chapter eight, verse twenty-eight”. A noisy room or a far‑away speaker can make the text less accurate. Place your iPhone closer to the speaker if you can. You can always add a verse yourself in your notes.

Which Bible translations are included?

World English Bible (WEB), Berean Standard Bible (BSB), King James Version (KJV) and American Standard Version (ASV). They are built into the app and work offline.

I’m Catholic. Are the deuterocanonical books included?

Yes. In Settings, set Church to Catholic. Pewside adds Tobit, Judith, Wisdom, Sirach, Baruch and 1–2 Maccabees, recognizes them in notes and live text, and names new recordings “Homily”.

Where are my recordings stored?

Only on your iPhone, and in your iCloud or computer backup if you use one. Pewside has no servers. See our Privacy Policy.

Can I record anywhere?

Please ask before recording where it’s required, and respect your church’s guidelines.

How do I restore Pro on a new iPhone?

Open Settings in the app and tap Restore Purchases. Make sure you’re signed in with the same Apple Account you used to subscribe.

How do I cancel my subscription or free trial?

Subscriptions are managed by Apple. On your iPhone, open the Settings app, tap your name, then Subscriptions, choose Pewside and tap Cancel. Pewside also sends you a reminder the day before a free trial ends.
