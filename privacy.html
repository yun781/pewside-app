Pewside

Privacy Policy

Effective October 14, 2026

The short version: Pewside does not collect, store, or share your personal data. Your recordings, text and notes stay on your iPhone. There are no accounts, no ads, no analytics and no tracking.

What the app does with your data

Recordings

When you tap Record, the app uses the microphone to record audio. Recordings are saved only inside the app on your iPhone. They are deleted when you delete them in the app or uninstall the app. If you back up your iPhone with iCloud or your computer, that backup may include the app’s data, as controlled by your device settings and Apple’s policies.

Live text

Speech is turned into text on your iPhone using Apple’s built-in speech recognition. Your audio and text are not sent to us or to anyone else. The first time, your iPhone may download a speech model from Apple; this download does not include any of your recordings.

Summaries and questions

Sermon summaries and small group questions are created on your iPhone using Apple Intelligence where available. No text or audio leaves your iPhone for these features.

Notes and the Bible

Your notes and settings are stored only on your iPhone. The Bible translations are built into the app, so reading and verse look-ups work without the internet.

Importing and sharing

When you import an audio file, you choose it in the system file picker, and the app can only see that file. When you share notes, a PDF or a recording, it is handed to the app you choose, such as Messages or Mail, and sent from your own device and account. We never see or receive it.

Reminders

If you start a free trial, the app may ask to send you a notification the day before it ends. The notification is scheduled on your iPhone. Nothing is sent to a server.

Purchases

Pewside Pro is processed by Apple through the App Store. We never receive your name, payment details or Apple Account information. The app only checks with Apple whether you have Pro so it can unlock Pro features.

Information we collect

None. We do not collect audio, text, usage data, diagnostics, identifiers or location, and we do not use third-party analytics or advertising SDKs.

Recording other people

You are responsible for following the laws and your church’s guidelines about recording where you are.

Children’s privacy

Pewside does not collect personal information from anyone, including children under 13.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we’ll update this page and the effective date above.
